Share your art

Made something kimono-inspired? Upload a photo of your piece and we'll feature it in the gallery with credit to you. Big photos are fine — we shrink them before uploading. Have a portfolio or website? Add the link and we'll show that too.

You can also tag @floridakimono on Instagram or post in our Facebook group.
